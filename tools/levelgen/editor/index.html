    Edits a tools/levelgen/*.json layout: the file tools/gen_level.py turns into the level. Export writes it back in the generator's own format; a layout you only open and export comes back byte for byte.

    • View: drag the plan to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom; the level buttons pick a floor (1-6); F fits.
    • Select (V): tap a room, doorway, opening, pad, camera or the start; drag it to move it. A room moves with its pads, objects, blocks and doorways; its yellow handles resize it.
    • Door (D): click a wall two rooms share. Then set its door and key rule in Selected.
    • Pad (P): click inside a room. Room (R): drag a box.
    • Del deletes, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo, Ctrl+S exports.
    • Checks run as you edit: the ones tagged gen_level are the generator's own and stop it; engine ones are the game's limits (32 cameras). Tap one to go to it.
    • Prop footprints near doorways need your extracted models: drop the footprints.json that preview.sh writes (build_preview/footprints.json) to check them here too.
    • To see it in the game: tools/levelgen/preview.sh my_layout.json (README.md).
